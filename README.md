# English translations of the 北师大版 primary-school mathematics textbooks

Twelve volumes (grades 1-6, 上册 and 下册), translated from Chinese into English by the `mathtrans`
pipeline on branch `claude/gracious-ride-64zqsv`: OCR of the scanned pages, DeepSeek translation with
a textbook glossary and conventions, three automatic QA rounds, and editable English text laid over the
cleaned page images. Page layout, pictures and colours are kept; page 2 (the QR-code page) is unchanged.

To download a file: open it, then use **Download raw file** (the download icon at the top right).

## Known limitations

- Sentences built around small pictures (for example 用○表示人 where the circle is a drawing) are
  translated without the picture or, when the OCR could not read the sentence around it, left in Chinese;
  these lines are listed as warnings in the QA report. This mostly affects grades 1-2.
- A few labels are left in Chinese because the English does not fit at a readable size; they are listed
  in the QA report as `layout_fit` ("left in source language").
- Some labels and speech bubbles are set in small type (2-3 % of text below 6 pt).
- Characters the OCR did not detect at all (rare: under the watermark, very small or decorative text)
  stay in Chinese.
- Answer boxes, formulas and numbers are left in the picture; their surroundings are translated.
- The QA reports list every remaining automatic finding per page for a human check.
